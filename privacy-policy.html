Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Information We Collect

I AM REDEMPTION (“IAR,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, donation information, event registration information, volunteer information, and other information you choose to provide.

We may also collect limited technical information when you use our website, such as browser type, device information, IP address, and website usage information.

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process donations and registrations.

  • Provide receipts and other transaction-related communications.

  • Communicate with you about I AM REDEMPTION programs, events, fundraising campaigns, volunteer opportunities, and organizational updates.

  • Send email or text message communications when you have provided the appropriate consent.

  • Respond to questions or requests.

  • Maintain and improve our website, programs, communications, and operations.

  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Text Messaging and SMS Consent

If you voluntarily opt in to receive text messages from I AM REDEMPTION, we may send you text messages regarding our nonprofit programs, events, fundraising campaigns, volunteer opportunities, community updates, and other organizational activities.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.

Your consent to receive text messages is not a condition of making a donation, participating in a program or event, volunteering, or otherwise engaging with I AM REDEMPTION.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won't be shared with any third parties.

Sharing of Information

We may share personal information with service providers that assist us in operating our website, processing donations, managing communications, administering programs, or providing other services on our behalf.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of I AM REDEMPTION or others.

Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Donations and Payment Information

Donations and other payments may be processed through third-party payment and fundraising platforms. I AM REDEMPTION does not necessarily receive or store complete payment-card information. Those providers process information according to their own privacy and security practices.

Email Communications

You may unsubscribe from marketing or promotional emails at any time by using the unsubscribe option provided in those communications. Certain transactional communications, such as donation receipts, may still be sent when necessary.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information in our possession. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and I AM REDEMPTION is not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.
