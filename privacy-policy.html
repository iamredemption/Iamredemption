Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

Information We Collect

I AM REDEMPTION (“IAR,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, donation information, event registration information, volunteer information, and other information you choose to provide.

We may also collect limited technical information when you use our website, such as browser type, device information, IP address, and website usage information.

If you use the I AM REDEMPTION mobile app (the “IAR App”), we also collect the information described in the section below.

The IAR App

This section applies only to the IAR App.

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your first and last name, email address, and password. Other members see only your first name and last initial. Your full name and email address are visible only to IAR administrators.

  • Age confirmation. The IAR App is for adults 18 and older. When you create an account, we use age information provided by the Apple App Store or Google Play, such as whether you are 18 or older, to confirm your eligibility. We do not receive or store your date of birth from these services. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use the IAR App, and if we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

  • Profile photo. If you add a profile photo, it is visible to members who share a group chat with you and to IAR administrators. You can change or remove it at any time, and administrators may remove a photo that does not follow our Terms & Conditions.

  • Event check-ins. When you check in to an event, we record which event you attended and when. IAR administrators use this information to understand participation in our programs.

  • Group chats. Messages you post in group chats are stored and visible to members of that chat and to IAR administrators. Messages are automatically checked against a list of blocked words. If you report a message, administrators can see the report, but the member you reported cannot see who reported them.

  • Photos you share. Photos you send using “Share photos” are saved to I AM REDEMPTION’s Google Drive with your name and the date. They may be used as described in our Terms & Conditions.

  • Feedback. Feedback you send about events and programs is anonymous. Your name and account are not saved with it.

  • Push notifications. If you allow notifications, we store your device’s notification address and the notification categories you choose. You can change your choices at any time in More → Notification settings, or turn notifications off in your phone’s settings.

  • Donations in the app. Donations made in the IAR App are processed by Givebutter. I AM REDEMPTION does not receive or store your payment-card details.

  • Videos and virtual meetings. Podcast episodes play through YouTube, and virtual meetings take place on Google Meet. Those services may collect information according to Google’s own privacy policy.

  • Deleting your account. You can delete your account at any time in Profile & settings. This permanently deletes your account, profile, profile photo, and settings. Anonymous feedback, and photos you already shared, are not linked to your account and are not removed automatically. To ask us to remove shared photos, contact us.

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process donations and registrations.

  • Provide receipts and other transaction-related communications.

  • Communicate with you about I AM REDEMPTION programs, events, fundraising campaigns, volunteer opportunities, and organizational updates.

  • Send email or text message communications when you have provided the appropriate consent.

  • Respond to questions or requests.

  • Operate the IAR App, including accounts, event check-ins, group chats, and notifications.

  • Maintain and improve our website, the IAR App, programs, communications, and operations.

  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Text Messaging and SMS Consent

If you voluntarily opt in to receive text messages from I AM REDEMPTION, we may send you text messages regarding our nonprofit programs, events, fundraising campaigns, volunteer opportunities, community updates, and other organizational activities.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.

Your consent to receive text messages is not a condition of making a donation, participating in a program or event, volunteering, or otherwise engaging with I AM REDEMPTION.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won't be shared with any third parties.

Sharing of Information

We may share personal information with service providers that assist us in operating our website and the IAR App, processing donations, managing communications, administering programs, or providing other services on our behalf.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of I AM REDEMPTION or others.

Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Donations and Payment Information

Donations and other payments may be processed through third-party payment and fundraising platforms. I AM REDEMPTION does not necessarily receive or store complete payment-card information. Those providers process information according to their own privacy and security practices.

Email Communications

You may unsubscribe from marketing or promotional emails at any time by using the unsubscribe option provided in those communications. Certain transactional communications, such as donation receipts, may still be sent when necessary.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information in our possession. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and I AM REDEMPTION is not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.
