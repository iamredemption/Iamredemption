The Partnership

Selected as an
Official Charity Partner.

Every year, the Austin Marathon selects a small group of nonprofits to stand alongside as Official Charity Partners through the Austin Marathon Gives program. I Am Redemption was chosen for the impact we make in the Austin recovery and running community, and for what we represent on race day: people who have rebuilt their lives showing up to move forward, one mile at a time.

This partnership puts IAR in front of thousands of runners, spectators, and volunteers across one of the largest events in Central Texas, and it directly supports our mission to turn pain into purpose.
